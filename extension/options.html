🎟 Coupon Wallet for Chrome

Shows a badge with your unused coupons whenever you're on a matching retailer's site.

Status: not connected
How connecting works
  1. The extension borrows the sign-in from the Coupon Wallet web app — no second account, no passwords.
  2. Click Connect: your wallet opens in a tab. Make sure you're signed in there (☁️ button).
  3. This page picks up the session automatically within ~30 seconds.
